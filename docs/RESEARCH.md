## Verified facts

- **Organiser:** Dubai Chamber of Digital Economy (Dubai Chambers). Launched 7 October 2026
  by H.H. Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum.
- **Categories (4):** Best Societal AI Agent · Best Personal AI Agent · Best Business AI
  Agent · Best Youth AI Agent (lead applicant under 21 at registration).
- **Prizes:** USD 150,000 grant per category winner (AED 550,000), no equity taken.
  One of the four winners is also crowned **Dubai AI Agent of the Year**, receiving an
  **additional USD 270,000+** (AED 1 million). Total pool: programme page states
  USD 750,000 in grants; launch press release states "above AED 2.5 million"
  (~USD 681,000). The two totals are not reconciled in public sources — see open items.
- **Eligibility:** Global, all ages, all backgrounds, no technical background required.
  Solo entries welcome at **all stages**. Teams up to 6 members. Multiple entries
  allowed, including across categories. Each team member registers individually.
- **Agent stage:** Idea, prototype, or early MVP at submission; must NOT already be a
  fully commercialised solution. Must be original — not copied from an existing
  repository. Grand Finalists must present a **fully functional, live AI Agent** on stage.
- **Submission components (4):** (1) working AI Agent prototype, (2) pitch deck
  (recommended 12–15 slides), (3) 2-minute demo video embedded in **Slide 4** of the
  deck, (4) 6-minute pitch video that includes the embedded demo.
  **The presenter's face MUST appear in the 6-minute pitch video.**
- **Submission window (official platform FAQ):** opens **4 December 2026**, closes
  **4 January 2027 at 11:59 AM Dubai time (GST, UTC+4)** = **14:59 WIB**.
  Submitted via the official Submission Portal on the Create AI Agent Learning Platform.
- **Prototype access:** must be accessible to judges and the championship team for
  testing (working link, test credentials, or approved alternative) and **must remain
  available until the end of the Championship**. No-code/low-code/coded solutions are
  evaluated equally. Clearly labelled simulated inputs are permitted, provided the
  agent performs a real, functioning workflow. Participants bear operating costs,
  including usage during judging.
- **Video format:** unlisted YouTube / Vimeo / Canva link, recorded in English, clear
  audio, preferably 1080p (16:9), viewable without requesting permission. Short cuts
  and sped-up waiting periods are permitted if clearly labelled.
- **Judging criteria:** Real Value & Problem Validation 20% · Technical Execution &
  Reliability 20% · Trust, Safety & Human Oversight 15% · Innovation & Defensibility
  15% · Business Viability & Scalability 20% · Pitch & Communication 10%.
- **Semi-finalists:** 24 teams, announced **29 January 2027**; they join the Tech
  Accelerator Programme (high-potential teams also invited).
- **Semi-Final Pitch Day:** March 2027 → 12 finalists announced March 2027.
- **Grand Final:** first week of May 2027, in person in Dubai. Finalists present and
  demonstrate a fully functional AI Agent — not an MVP — live.
- **Resubmission:** an existing submission cannot be edited, but may be resubmitted;
  organisers recommend submitting only when 100% ready.
- **Mentorship:** Mentor Circle (Business, Ideation, Technical Development, Pitching),
  30-minute 1:1 sessions, November–December 2026, optional.
- **Data rule:** the solution must protect personal, confidential and organisational
  data and prevent information leakage.
- **Training participation:** recommended, not mandatory.

## NOT FOUND / unresolved — do not treat as fact

1. **Exact registration closing day: NOT FOUND on official sources.** The official
   programme page timeline says only "January 2027". The 7 October launch release
   said "December 2026". No official source gives an exact day. One third-party
   aggregator (fundsforngos.org) claims 31 January 2027 — unverified, do not rely on
   it. **Mitigation: register as early as possible (December 2026) and confirm the
   date inside the registration account.**
2. **Whether the AED 1M Agent of the Year prize includes or adds to the category
   grant:** the platform FAQ says "additional grand prize of USD 270,000+"; the press
   release arithmetic is ambiguous. Treat as "additional" per the FAQ, confirm in terms.
3. **Total pool figure:** USD 750,000 (programme FAQ) vs "above AED 2.5M / ~USD 681K"
   (press release). Use per-category figures in the pitch, not the total.
4. **Exact Grand Final day and Semi-Final Pitch Day:** month/week only ("first week
   of May 2027", "March 2027").

## Secondary-source claims to verify in the registration terms (not yet confirmed)
