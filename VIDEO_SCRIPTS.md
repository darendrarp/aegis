# AEGIS — Video Scripts

> Two videos are required. The 2-minute demo is embedded in Slide 4 of the pitch
> deck AND played inside the 6-minute pitch video.
> **The presenter's face MUST appear in the 6-minute pitch video** (official
> requirement). That video is recorded by the user (adt) — this file provides the
> script and storyboard only.
>
> **Hard rule:** no AI-generated face, no face-swap, no deepfake, no impersonation.
> The user records himself, in English, 1080p (16:9), clear audio, unlisted
> YouTube/Vimeo/Canva link.
>
> Short cuts and sped-up waiting periods are permitted if clearly labelled.

Format: screen capture of the live AEGIS prototype, with concise voiceover (the
user's own voice, or a neutral narrator voice — voiceover only, no face needed).
On-screen captions for every key moment. Background music: none or very low.

## VIDEO 2 — Pitch Video (6:00, face on camera — recorded by the user)

Structure (per official guidance): play the embedded 2-minute demo, then use the
remaining ~4 minutes for problem, solution, business opportunity, and vision.
**Face must appear** — recommended: picture-in-picture (face in corner) during the
demo playback, full-frame for the pitch segments.

### Setup checklist (for the user)
- [ ] Quiet room, plain background, face well lit (window or lamp in front, not behind).
- [ ] Phone/camera at eye level, 1080p, 16:9 landscape, stable (tripod or stack of books).
- [ ] Clear audio: phone mic is fine in a quiet room; speak clearly, medium pace.
- [ ] Record in **English**. Teleprompter: script on a laptop just behind/below the camera.
- [ ] Record in 2–3 takes per segment; edit the best takes together. Total target: 5:45–6:00.
- [ ] Upload as **unlisted** YouTube/Vimeo/Canva link; verify it plays without sign-in.

### Script

**[0:00–0:25] OPENING — full frame, face on camera**
> "Hi, I'm [name], and I build AI agents that protect people.
> Last year, crypto scams stole seventeen billion dollars — and the victims were
> mostly ordinary people: first-time users, retirees, migrant workers sending money
> home. Once the money is gone, it's gone — there's no chargeback, no fraud
> department, no undo button.
> I built AEGIS to stop the theft before it happens."

**[0:25–2:25] DEMO — the 2-minute demo video plays (face in picture-in-picture corner)**
> (No new narration — the demo has its own voiceover. Before it starts, one line:)
> "Let me show you — this is AEGIS stopping a real scam, live."
> *(Play Video 1 in full.)*

**[2:25–3:15] PROBLEM + WHY NOW — full frame**
> "Scammers already use AI — their campaigns earn four and a half times more than
> before. And the tools we give victims are just warning popups written in developer
> language. A red flag doesn't stop anyone from signing.
> AEGIS is different. It's an agent, not a scanner. It investigates like a security
> researcher, explains like a friend, and acts — it blocks the dangerous signature,
> removes the risky approval, and warns the whole network. And it does it in plain
> language, in English, Arabic, or Indonesian — for the people who need it most."

**[3:15–4:15] HOW IT WORKS + TRUST — full frame, cut to 1–2 architecture stills**
> "Here's the important part: AEGIS never holds your keys and never moves your
> money. The AI plans and explains — but every verdict is verified by deterministic
> checks against live blockchain data. If it can't verify something is safe, it says
> so. It fails closed, because a false 'all clear' is the one mistake that costs
> people their savings.
> In our benchmark of one hundred labelled contracts, AEGIS got over ninety percent
> right — and never once called a scam safe."
> *(Fill in final benchmark numbers at record time — only real, measured figures.)*

**[4:15–5:15] BUSINESS — full frame**
> "There are over five hundred and sixty million crypto owners in the world, and
> almost none of them have protection. AEGIS is free for basic checks, five dollars
> a month for full protection — and wallets and exchanges pay for our API, because
> protecting their users is now a competitive advantage.
> The grant funds the build-out to a fully functional agent, the threat-intelligence
> network, and establishing AEGIS in Dubai — the city that wants to be the world's
> safest place to build with crypto. Protection is part of that story, and I want to
> build it here, for the world."

**[5:15–6:00] CLOSE — full frame, direct to camera**
> "Seventeen billion dollars a year. That's not a statistic — that's retirement
> savings, remittances, first investments, gone. The technology to stop it exists.
> It just needs to be in the hands of the people being targeted.
> That's AEGIS. Thank you."
> *(Hold a beat, slight smile, end card: AEGIS logo + prototype link.)*

**Word count check:** ~430 words of pitch narration ≈ 3:00–3:20 at natural pace,
plus the 2:00 demo and transitions = ~5:45–6:00 total. Rehearse with a timer;
cut from the business section first if running long — never cut the demo or the close.
